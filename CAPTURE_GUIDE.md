# Surface Capture Guide

Useful source photographs are:

- straight-on where possible
- high resolution and free of motion blur
- captured across multiple adjacent regions
- accompanied by a measurement reference or known dimension
- captured dry and wet when those states matter
- free of identifiable private information

Preserve the original file. Record approximate physical dimensions, lighting conditions, location context, and attribution before processing. Avoid harsh shadows unless the shadow itself is part of the material being captured.
